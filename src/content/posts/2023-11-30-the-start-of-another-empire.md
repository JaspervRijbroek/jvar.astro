Recently, my spouse and I embarked on an exhilarating adventure, trading our cozy house in the Netherlands for the sun-drenched beaches of Spain! After much thought and deliberation, we made a strategic choice to keep our official registration at our former residence, and I must say—this decision has really paid off. However, we now face a significant challenge: selling our beloved Dutch home.

Keeping my registration in the Netherlands has been a lifesaver, allowing me to maintain my status as a Dutch tax resident, which is essential for my role at my current company. But here’s the kicker—once we sell the house, I’ll have to bid farewell to my job, which means I’m on the hunt for new career opportunities.

After some heartfelt discussions with my spouse, we’ve decided that diving into freelance work is my best bet for the future. I used to run my own business, but I closed it down a while back. Now, I’m thrilled to reignite that entrepreneurial spirit right here in sunny Spain, and I’ve got some ambitious plans lined up that I’m confident will take off!

## The Freedom of Freelancing: Why It's Worth Considering
I often get asked why someone would choose to embark on a freelance journey. The answer is rooted in the complexities of European Union regulations, which can make it tricky for individuals to be officially employed in one country while living and working in another. Sure, there are alternatives—like having your employer set up a company in your home country or using an employer of record—but let's be honest, those options can be quite costly. 

This is where freelancing shines as a compelling solution. It not only offers the flexibility to work from anywhere but also allows you to take control of your own business without getting bogged down by hefty expenses. So, if you’re looking for a way to break free from conventional employment constraints, diving into the world of freelancing could be the perfect path for you!

This new chapter undoubtedly comes with its share of hurdles, but I’m ready to face them head-on. My previous experience running a restaurant has armed me with valuable insights into the business landscape. This time, however, I understand how crucial it is to tackle invoicing, contracts, and insurance with a keen eye for detail and a splash of professionalism.

In my upcoming blog post, I’ll be diving deep into the ins and outs of launching a business in Spain, sharing the steps I’m taking to ensure everything goes smoothly. A key part of my strategy involves bringing on a gestor—a professional who will help me navigate my company's tax returns. I know that managing those complexities on my own could be daunting, especially since I want to focus my energy on creating and sending invoices. 

So, keep an eye out for my insights and updates as I embark on this thrilling new journey! Can't wait to share the adventure with you!
