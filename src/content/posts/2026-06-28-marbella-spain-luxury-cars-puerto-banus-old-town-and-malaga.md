This long weekend, we embarked on an unforgettable adventure to the stunning coastal paradise of Marbella, Spain. Renowned for its opulence, many say Puerto Banus is the crown jewel of the region, with its jaw-dropping yachts and glitzy supercars. Eager to experience this spectacle firsthand, we set our sights on discovering what Marbella had to offer.

As always, we hit the road, and after a scenic drive filled with anticipation, we finally arrived at our hotel on the outskirts of Marbella. Once we settled in, the allure of Puerto Banus drew us in. The moment we stepped into the harbor, my son and I were wide-eyed, captivated by an incredible array of cars. Ferraris, Lamborghinis, Rolls-Royces, Porsches – and to our delight, even a stunning Koenigsegg Jesko in the showroom! 

It turned out to be a brilliant decision, as my wife equally enjoyed the day, soaking up the glamor around us. We soon found ourselves chuckling at how we were somewhat underdressed among the crowd of yacht owners and sophisticated visitors, but that only added to the fun of our experience.

After a thrilling day filled with sights and sounds, we returned to our hotel, ready to unwind and recharge for the adventures that lay ahead.

On day two, we kicked things off with breakfast at a quaint local café that boasted both affordability and flavor, just a stone's throw from the beach. With our bellies full, we strolled along the picturesque promenade, soaking in the sun, before venturing into the enchanting local park – a hidden gem that truly lives up to its rave reviews. The Old Town was a highlight as well; we lost ourselves in its charming alleyways, perfect for leisurely exploration.

Later, we made our way to La Canada Shopping Center, where we stocked up on breakfast goodies, snacks for the road, and indulged in a bit of shopping – a fabulous way to close out the day.

Our final morning began with an early check-out as we had one last destination on our itinerary: vibrant Malaga. We spent the day meandering through the city's beautiful streets and soaking up its rich culture, savoring a delicious lunch as we reflected on our incredible getaway. With our hearts full and cameras laden with memories, we finally hit the road back home, already dreaming of our next adventure.

We can't wait to share our next thrilling adventure with you! Stay tuned for the excitement ahead!

Hasta Luega, mis amigos!
