Have you ever heard of Villajoyosa? If not, you're in for a treat! My recent visit to this charming town in Spain left me absolutely thrilled. And trust me, the best part is that it's super easy to get to - they even have an outdoor escalator!

What makes this little gem so special is that it's off the beaten path. The highlight of my trip was strolling down the promenade, where every house is painted in the most vibrant colors, and the beach is just a hop and a skip away. The white sand and gentle incline into the sea make it the perfect place for kids to play too.

But wait, it gets better! Take the escalator up to the higher part of town and you'll find a park with tons of fun activities for kids. While they're having a blast, you can soak in the breathtaking views.

And when it's time to grab a bite to eat, head back down to the promenade. The food selection is amazing, with something to satisfy every palate. Trust me, you won't be disappointed!

So what are you waiting for? Add Villajoyosa to your travel bucket list and experience the magic for yourself.
