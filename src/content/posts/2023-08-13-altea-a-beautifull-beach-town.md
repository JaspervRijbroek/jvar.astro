Let's explore the charming coastal town of Altea, located in the province of Valencia. This town is definitely worth a visit when you're in the Valencian Community, and it's only an hour's drive from Valencia.

Now, I must warn you that the old town might not be the easiest to explore with a stroller but trust me, it's totally worth it for the beautiful experience you'll have. You won't want to miss climbing the stairs to the old town and wandering through the narrow, winding streets.

While you're exploring the town, make sure to take a moment to catch your breath and enjoy the breathtaking view of the sea from one of the balconies on the main square. After that, why not treat yourself to a refreshing drink or a little snack?

Once you've had your fill of the old town, head to the beach promenade, where you can find some lovely souvenirs and enjoy some delicious ice cream while taking in the beautiful beach scenery.
